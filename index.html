01 / ABOUT

Engineering through building and experimentation.

I'm a mechanical engineer pursuing an M.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, building on a B.E. in Biomedical Engineering from Boston University. My work sits at the intersection of robotics, mechatronics, and medical devices — from designing master–slave endovascular robotic platforms and closed-loop motor control systems to running human-factors studies that quantify how operators interact with surgical robots.

I'm currently a Member of Technical Staff at an early-stage medical-device startup and a graduate researcher at CMU's CERLAB, where I develop robotic platforms for endovascular navigation and study how motion scaling and interface design affect surgical performance. I'm looking for opportunities in medical robotics, mechatronic device development, and human-centered engineering.
